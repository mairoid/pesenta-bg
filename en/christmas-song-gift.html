Frequently asked questions

Can you deliver on Christmas Eve?
Yes, within 24 hours of payment. In the busiest week, order a few days early to be safe.

Yes. We write songs in English, Bulgarian, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Greek, Serbian, Macedonian, Romanian, Turkish, Russian, Ukrainian and Arabic — or a mix. Pick the language in the order form.
How fast will I get it?
Within 24 hours of payment you get the MP3 and the lyrics by email. If you need it sooner, write to sales@pesenta.bg before you pay — when we can, it's ready the same day.
